## Platform

web

## Stack

Inferred for this session: static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript so the site can be opened or hosted anywhere without a build step.

## Users

Inferred: homeowners and small-premise operators around Kuala Lumpur who have an active plumbing problem and need a trustworthy local technician quickly.

## Product Purpose

Ahmad Plumbing presents core plumbing services, operating areas, proof of workmanship, and immediate contact routes. Success means a visitor quickly understands the service and starts a WhatsApp conversation or phone call.

## Positioning

Inferred from the supplied references: responsive local plumbing help with neat workmanship, transparent communication, and practical coverage around Kuala Lumpur.

## Operating Context

Visitors are likely on a phone, may be dealing with a leak or blockage, and need to share a photo of the issue before booking.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single-page Malay-language marketing site.
- Primary actions: WhatsApp and phone call.
- Service areas shown: Taman Melawati, Setapak, Wangsa Maju, Ampang, Gombak, and Sentul.
- The phone number and testimonial are treated as editable sample content because no verified business details were available in the workspace.
- Inferred static stack; deployment target remains open.
