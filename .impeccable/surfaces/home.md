## THESIS

Ahmad Plumbing should feel like the capable local technician already on the way: direct, calm, visibly equipped, and easy to contact during a stressful household problem.

## OWN-WORLD

The world is built from real plumbing materials and operating rituals: deep navy workwear, cyan pipe-line accents, chrome, wet tile, service tools, a warm residential interior, and the bright orange urgency of an immediate contact action.

## STORY

The visitor sees the technician at work, learns how the team behaves, recognizes the service they need, confirms local coverage, sees illustrative workmanship detail, and reaches a final WhatsApp or phone action.

## FIRST VIEWPORT

A full-bleed repair scene shows the job already in progress. Large Malay copy and two clear contact actions occupy the dark negative space, while the plumber and working sink remain immediately legible.

## FORM
